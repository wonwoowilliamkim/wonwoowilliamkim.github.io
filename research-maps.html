Korea Policy Evaluation — Research Map
D3.js · Force Graph YAML Ontology Top Journals: AER · QJE · JPE · REStud · Econometrica

An interactive knowledge graph of top-journal papers for Korean policy evaluation. Organizes papers by policy area — fertility, education, housing, labor markets, social insurance, and regional policy — and tags each paper as a Korea-specific study 🇰🇷, a comparable international template 🌐, or a core methodology paper 📐. Designed to answer: "Which papers should I reference for this Korean policy question?"

ML-to-Econ Methods · Research Map
D3.js · Force Graph YAML Ontology From intro data mining to the 2026 frontier · 10 method families

An Obsidian-style knowledge graph of machine-learning and computer-science methods and how economists use them. It runs from intro data mining (decision trees, k-means, cross-validation) to the current frontier (causal machine learning, prediction-powered inference, performative prediction, tabular foundation models). Each technique is tagged with a level and how far economics has adopted it, and links to the papers that brought it into the field.
