Wonwoo (William) Kim

PhD Student in Economics @ UC Irvine

Research interests: Public, Labor, and Health Economics

I am especially interested in why individuals respond differently to the same public policy, and how those differences should reshape optimal policy design. I aim to study this heterogeneity in labor and health settings amid declining fertility.

I plan to combine quasi-experimental identification with machine learning methods to estimate who responds to a policy and by how much. These estimates can inform targeting, and the sufficient statistics framework can translate them into welfare effects.
