Fields of Interest

Public Economics Labor Economics Health Economics
  • Lost in Translation or Strategic Choice? LLM Evidence on Cross-Listed Risk Disclosure Asymmetry
    Work in Progress with Siwon Lee (Washington University in St. Louis)
    Developing an LLM-based framework to compare home-country and SEC risk disclosures for U.S.-listed Asian firms, documenting disclosure asymmetries across matched filing pairs while mitigating cross-lingual measurement bias.
  • Return Payments and the Ban Paradox in Asylum Policy
    Work in Progress with Prof. Robert Dur (Erasmus University Rotterdam)
    Developing a sequential screening model of return payments for asylum seekers, and extending time-inconsistency frameworks to analyze how credibility constraints shape migration decisions.
  • Optimal Taxation of E-Cigarette Products
    Work in Progress with Prof. Hans van Kippersluis (Erasmus University Rotterdam)
    Building a welfare-based model to determine the optimal e-cigarette excise tax for the Netherlands, incorporating cross-border arbitrage and estimating Dutch-specific elasticities through spatial variation analysis.
  • Cycling Infrastructure, Weather, and Outdoor Crime: Evidence from Rotterdam
    Work in Progress with Jun Lim (Erasmus University Rotterdam)
    Exploiting city-wide weather shocks interacted with neighborhood cycling infrastructure quality to test whether cycling generates informal street-level guardianship, finding that better infrastructure reduces theft from and of motor vehicles across 77 Rotterdam neighborhoods.
